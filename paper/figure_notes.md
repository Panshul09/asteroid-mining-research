# Figure notes

- Figure 1: Shows the modeled break-even price across feedstock-throughput levels for four assumed resource fractions (5%, 10%, 15%, and 20%).
- Figure 2: Shows the modeled break-even price across combinations of resource fraction and extraction efficiency.
- Figure 3: Shows one-at-a-time local sensitivity around the baseline, with nominal +/-20% changes. The model caps increased percentage-valued inputs at 99% when necessary, so the positive change can be smaller than 20% for inputs near the upper bound.
- Figure 4: Shows how modeled break-even price changes as the fixed mission/development cost changes while other baseline inputs remain constant.

All figures are scenario-model outputs, not observations from an actual asteroid-mining mission.
